Template-driven platform

Four hubs.
One operating model.

A new hub isn’t a new application. Each workstream is a template + module configuration on the same governed platform, so proven capabilities compound instead of being rebuilt.

Inspection & maintenance

OHL Inspection

Assets, incidents, maintenance boards, actions, risks and hourly automated ingest for overhead-line inspection programmes.

SEDO / compliance

Monthly compliance cycle, worker document gaps, supplier boards, outstanding items and POPIA-aware status tracking.

Finance operations

Payment Processing

Reconciles POs, invoices and supplier documents; flags expired POs and expected invoices that never arrived.

Platform operations

Hub Dev

The platform tracks itself: git-derived changelog, backlog, release checklist and focus signals for stale or broken work.

Trust & governance

Serious workflows need visible control.

Project Hub is deliberately conservative at the point where AI becomes operational truth. Proposals remain proposals until a person owns the decision.
