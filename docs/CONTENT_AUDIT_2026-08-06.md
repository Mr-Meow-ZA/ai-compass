# Content audit — 6 August 2026

## Review finding

The balanced-platform rebuild restored the correct product structure, but the library was still strongest in agents, open source and technical workflows. It had weaker coverage for the work most new users encounter first: documents, spreadsheets, meetings, quality evaluation, multimodal input and team governance.

## Gaps addressed in version 0.6.2

### Everyday work

Added a practical guide for documents, spreadsheets and meetings. It includes source-pack preparation, spreadsheet control totals, meeting action registers and a measurable pilot method.

### Evaluation

Added a six-part scorecard covering correctness, evidence, completeness, usefulness, safety and reproducibility. This gives every future guide and automation a reusable quality framework.

### Architecture choice

Added a decision guide distinguishing rule-based automation, AI-assisted workflows and agents. It prioritises the simplest reliable pattern and introduces authority budgets, approval gates and shadow-mode pilots.

### Governance and security

Added a lightweight governance starter kit with an AI inventory, three risk tiers, release gates, prompt-injection controls and continuous review.

### Multimodal literacy

Added a guide to text, image, audio and video workflows with modality-specific verification, privacy and accessibility practices.

### Supporting collections

- Learning paths increased from 3 to 5.
- Practical tips increased from 8 to 20.
- Reference terms increased from 10 to 22.
- Goal-based homepage entry points increased from 4 to 6.

## Remaining high-value gaps

1. A dedicated prompt and workflow template library with copyable, versioned artefacts.
2. Sector-specific starter packs for education, small business, operations and creative work.
3. A data and spreadsheet learning path with downloadable practice files.
4. More complete coverage of image, audio and video creation workflows.
5. Source freshness indicators and superseded-content status across all guides.
6. Shared progress, bookmarks and community review once a backend is approved.

## Editorial rule

New content should fill a user-job or decision gap. Daily news volume must not displace evergreen guides, reference material, practical tips or maintained comparisons.
