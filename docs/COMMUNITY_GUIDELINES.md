# AI Compass Community Guidelines

AI Compass is building a community for people learning, using and building with AI. The goal is not to create the loudest AI forum. The goal is to create a place where a beginner can ask a sincere question, an experienced practitioner can share a useful implementation lesson, and both can tell the difference between evidence, experience and opinion.

## The standard

### Be useful

Ask specific questions. Add context. Explain what you tried. When answering, try to move the discussion forward rather than simply showing what you know.

### Challenge ideas, not people

Disagreement is welcome. Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory abuse, intimidation and dog-piling are not.

### Protect private and company information

Do not post passwords, API keys, access codes, private keys, customer information, personal data, confidential company material or internal documents you do not have permission to share. Redact examples before posting them.

If a workflow depends on sensitive information, describe the shape of the problem rather than publishing the sensitive material itself.

### Separate fact, experience and opinion

When making a factual claim that could materially affect someone's decision, link to a strong source when practical. Primary documentation, research papers, standards and maintainer-owned repositories are preferred.

Personal experience is valuable. Label it as experience rather than presenting one implementation as a universal rule.

### Disclose meaningful AI assistance

AI-assisted drafting is allowed. If AI generated a substantial technical answer, benchmark interpretation, review or recommendation, say so when that context would help readers judge the contribution. The person posting remains responsible for checking the content.

Do not flood the forum with unreviewed model output.

### No spam or disguised promotion

Do not mass-post links, referral codes, affiliate promotions, repetitive launch announcements or generic marketing copy. If you are connected to a product you recommend, disclose that connection.

Relevant project showcases are welcome in **What I built** when they explain what was built, why, what worked, what failed and what others can learn.

### Keep high-stakes advice appropriately cautious

AI Compass is an educational community, not a substitute for qualified legal, medical, financial, safety or cybersecurity professionals. High-stakes claims should be sourced and framed with their limits.

### Respect intellectual property

Share your own material, material you have permission to share, or short attributed excerpts where appropriate. Do not repost paid courses, books, articles, proprietary prompts, private repositories or copyrighted datasets without permission.

## How moderation works

Community content is user-generated and remains separate from AI Compass editorial guidance.

Moderators may remove spam, abuse, unsafe content, privacy violations, unlawful material, repeated off-topic posting or content that deliberately misrepresents its source. Threads may be locked when productive discussion is no longer possible.

Moderation actions are recorded separately from editorial content. Popularity, likes or helpful votes do not make a community claim an AI Compass editorial fact.

Members can report a thread or reply using the in-product **Report** control. Reports are reviewed by moderators; reporting something does not automatically remove it.

## Helpful answers and accepted answers

A **Helpful** vote means a reader found a reply useful. It is not a factual certification.

An **Accepted answer** means the thread author selected that reply as the answer that best addressed their question. It does not convert the answer into AI Compass editorial guidance.

## Public profile and post visibility

Forum discussions are designed to be readable publicly. Usernames, display names, experience levels, bios and published forum contributions should therefore be treated as public information.

Email addresses used for authentication are not displayed on public community profiles.

Do not put information in your username, display name, bio or posts that you do not want publicly visible.

## Community identity

AI Compass should reward useful contribution rather than volume. Reputation features, if added later, should favour constructive answers, demonstrated work and consistent community behaviour—not posting frequency.

The editorial team may update these guidelines as the community grows. Significant changes should be documented rather than silently changing the rules after the fact.
